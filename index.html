Small Repairs, Stronger Relationships

冲突之后如何用小小的修复行动重建信任，让关系更有韧性。

B2–C1约 10 分钟2026-09-24
01

Vocabulary

生词和重点词语

repair attempt n. phr.

修复尝试；冲突中或冲突后缓和关系的言语或行动

A sincere apology can be an effective repair attempt.

rupture n.

关系破裂；信任或联系的中断

Even a minor misunderstanding can cause a temporary rupture.

goodwill n.

善意；友好合作的意愿

Her gentle joke restored some goodwill between them.

take accountability v. phr.

承担责任；承认自己行为造成的影响

Taking accountability is more useful than making excuses.

defensiveness n.

防御心理；因感到受批评而急于自我辩护

Defensiveness can prevent people from hearing each other clearly.

make amends v. phr.

弥补过错；通过行动修复伤害

He tried to make amends by changing the behavior that had hurt her.

relational resilience n. phr.

关系韧性；关系在压力或冲突后恢复的能力

Honest repair builds relational resilience over time.

02

Reading

英语短文

The Skill of Repairing a Relationship

Healthy relationships are not conflict-free. Friends, partners, and colleagues sometimes interrupt each other, forget important details, or react too sharply. What matters is not whether a rupture ever occurs, but whether the people involved know how to repair it. A repair attempt can be as simple as saying, “That came out wrong. Can I try again?”

Effective repair begins with accountability. Instead of defending your intention, acknowledge the impact of your behavior: “I did not mean to embarrass you, but I can see that my comment did.” This does not require you to accept every accusation. It means showing enough curiosity to understand the other person’s experience before explaining your own.

Timing also matters. When defensiveness is high, even a thoughtful apology may not be heard. A short pause can help both people become calmer, provided that they agree to return to the conversation. Later, a warm tone, a small gesture of goodwill, or a specific apology can reopen the connection.

Finally, making amends involves more than saying sorry. If the same problem keeps happening, trust depends on changed behavior. Had you listened more carefully, the conflict might have ended sooner—but the next conversation is still an opportunity to do better. Relationships become resilient not because people never fail each other, but because they learn to notice harm, repair it, and grow from it.

03

Grammar & Patterns

语法与句型

What matters is not whether A, but whether B.

重要的不是是否 A，而是是否 B。用于重新界定讨论重点；whether 后接完整从句。

Example: What matters is not whether you disagree, but whether you remain respectful.

Had + 主语 + 过去分词, 主语 + might/would have + 过去分词.

这是省略 if 的第三条件句倒装，表示与过去事实相反的假设及其可能结果，语气较正式。

Example: Had I paused before replying, I might have avoided the argument.
