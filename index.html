Daily English · 旅行住宿：协商换房与确认费用

A Quieter Room Without a Bigger Bill

两天背诵版：4段短文，5个词组，1个语法点。第1天背前两段，第2天背后两段并串联复述；约10分钟含朗读与练习。

B2–C1约 10 分钟2026-10-07
01

Vocabulary

生词和重点词语

disturb my sleep phr.

打扰我的睡眠

The noise is disturbing my sleep.

at no extra charge phr.

不另收费

Could I move at no extra charge?

exceed my budget phr.

超出我的预算

An upgrade would exceed my budget.

subject to availability phr.

视空余情况而定

Room changes are subject to availability.

confirm in writing phr.

以书面形式确认

Could you confirm the arrangement in writing?

02

Reading

英语短文 · 两天背诵版

A Practical Request at Reception

I’m staying in a room facing the street, and the noise is disturbing my sleep. I understand that the hotel is busy, but would it be possible to move to a quieter room?

I’d appreciate it if you could arrange this at no extra charge. I booked three nights, and an upgrade would exceed my budget. A smaller room would be fine, provided it is quiet.

I realise that room changes are subject to availability. If nothing is available tonight, I’d be happy to move tomorrow. Could I leave my luggage at reception while the new room is being prepared?

Thank you for your help. Could you confirm the arrangement in writing, including the room rate and whether breakfast is included? That would help avoid any misunderstanding.

03

Grammar & Patterns

语法与句型

I’d appreciate it if you could + 动词原形…

表示“如果您能……，我将不胜感激”，用于委婉请求。I’d = I would；这里 could 并非过去时间，而是让语气更礼貌。

Example: I’d appreciate it if you could confirm the room rate.

04

Quick Practice

快速练习

  1. 替换练习用 I’d appreciate it if you could… 请求酒店以书面确认安排。
  2. 两天背诵第1天：理解并背诵前两段（说明问题、提出请求）。第2天：背诵后两段（协商时间、确认安排），再不看原文串联复述。可将 street 换成 lift、noise 换成 music，练习迁移。
