Vocabulary

生词和重点词语

emotional bandwidth n.

情绪容量；处理情绪或压力的精力

I don’t have the emotional bandwidth for a serious talk tonight.

set boundaries v. phr.

设定界限；明确自己能接受和不能接受的事

Setting boundaries can protect a healthy relationship.

validate v.

认可某人的感受或经历是合理、真实的

You can validate her feelings without agreeing with her decision.

misinterpret v.

underlying issue n. phr.

02

Reading

英语短文

When Communication Feels Difficult

Communication often becomes difficult not because people do not care, but because they are tired, defensive, or afraid of being misunderstood. A short reply may be misinterpreted as coldness, while silence may look like rejection. Before reacting, it helps to ask what the other person meant rather than assuming the worst.

When someone is upset, trying to solve the problem immediately is not always helpful. First, validate the feeling: “I can see why that hurt you.” Validation does not mean that you agree with every conclusion. It simply shows that you are listening and that the other person’s experience matters.

Healthy communication also requires boundaries. If you lack the emotional bandwidth to continue, you can say, “I want to discuss this, but I need twenty minutes to calm down.” Respecting that boundary gives both people space to think. A pause should not be used as punishment; it should be a clear, respectful step toward returning to the conversation.

Finally, focus on the underlying issue and use language that de-escalates the conflict. Instead of blaming—“You never listen”—try describing your experience: “I felt ignored when I was interrupted.” Good communication is reciprocal: both people listen, clarify, and take responsibility. The goal is not to win, but to understand each other well enough to move forward.

03

Grammar & Patterns

语法与句型

It is not that A, but that B.

不是因为 A，而是因为 B。用于纠正常见误解，并指出更准确的原因。A、B 后通常接完整从句。

Example: It is not that she does not care, but that she needs time to process her feelings.

Instead of + doing..., try + doing...

与其做……，不如试着做……。instead of 和 try 后都使用动名词，适合提出温和、具体的替代方案。

Example: Instead of assuming the worst, try asking a calm question.
