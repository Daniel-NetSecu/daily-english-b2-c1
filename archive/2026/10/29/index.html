Daily English · 生活英语：预约家电维修并确认报价与授权范围

Agreeing on a Repair Before the Work Begins

维修开始前，先把费用与范围说清楚

B2–C1约 10 分钟2026-10-29
01

Vocabulary

生词和重点词语

under warranty phr.

在保修期内；是否免费维修仍取决于具体保修条款。

Check whether the appliance is still under warranty before booking a repair.

callout fee n. phr.

上门服务费；不一定包含人工或零件费用。

Does the callout fee include the first thirty minutes of labour?

a written estimate n. phr.

书面估价；预计费用，不应自动理解为固定报价。

Could you send me a written estimate before starting the work?

authorise repairs v. phr.

授权进行维修；明确同意维修范围。

Please do not order any parts until I authorise the repairs.

go ahead phr. v.

开始或继续做某事；此处表示获得许可后开展维修。

You can go ahead once we have agreed on the total cost.

the scope of the job n. phr.

工作范围；具体包含哪些项目。

Let us confirm the scope of the job before you begin.

match the agreed work v. phr.

与双方约定的工作内容一致。

The items on the invoice should match the agreed work.

run a test cycle v. phr.

运行一次测试程序；例如测试洗衣机是否正常工作。

Please run a test cycle so we can check for leaks.

02

Reading

英语短文

Agreeing on a Repair Before the Work Begins｜空白分词：107 / 109 / 109；共325词

When the washing machine stopped halfway through a cycle, Maya resisted the temptation to book the first repair service she found. Instead, she wrote down the model number, photographed the error message, and checked whether the appliance was still under warranty. On the phone, she described what had happened without claiming to know the cause. The receptionist offered a visit the following afternoon, but Maya wanted to clarify the costs first. 'Does the callout fee include any labour, or is it only for diagnosing the problem?' she asked. Getting that distinction clear would help her compare the service with another company's offer and avoid an unexpected bill.

The technician explained that the initial fee covered the visit and diagnosis, while parts and repair work would cost extra. Maya asked for a written estimate before authorising any repairs. 'Provided that the total stays within the agreed limit, you can go ahead,' she said. 'If you discover another fault, please contact me before doing additional work.' She also asked whether the replacement part was available and how long the repair was likely to take. These questions were not intended to challenge the technician's expertise. They established a practical agreement about the scope of the job, the likely cost, and the point at which further approval would be needed.

After replacing a worn component, the technician ran a short test cycle and checked for leaks. Maya watched the machine drain properly, then asked what she should do if the same problem returned. Before paying, she checked that the invoice matched the agreed work and requested a receipt showing the part that had been fitted. She saved the documents alongside the original estimate, making any future follow-up easier. The repair had required more than simply arranging a visit: it had involved describing symptoms, setting a spending limit, and confirming the result. Clear questions had helped both people understand their responsibilities without turning a routine service call into a confrontation.

03

Grammar

语法与句型

Provided that + 主语 + 一般现在时, 主句（can / will + 动词原形）

provided that 表示“只要；条件是”，用于明确许可或承诺的前提，语气比 if 更强调条件限制。正文中，允许开始维修的前提是总价不超过约定上限。常见错误：表示将来的条件从句通常不用 will；不要写 Provided that the total will stay…。也不要把 provided that 误解为无条件同意。

Example: Provided that the total stays within the agreed limit, you can go ahead.

两天背诵建议：从描述问题到确认结果

第1天：背第1段及第2段前三句，按“收集型号与症状→问清上门费→提出有条件授权”复述；用自己的家电替换 washing machine。第2天：先回忆前一天的费用问题，再背第2段剩余内容与第3段，按“额外维修先联系→测试→核对账单→保存凭证”串联全文，最后脱稿模拟电话对话。

Example: Provided that the repair costs no more than the estimate, you can go ahead.

04

Practice

快速练习

  1. 理解1. What did the initial fee cover, and what would cost extra? 2. What should the technician do if another fault is discovered? 3. Why did Maya save the documents?
  2. 词语Fill the gaps using expressions from the vocabulary: (a) Is the appliance still ___? (b) Please send a ___ before starting. (c) We need to confirm the ___ of the job. (d) You can ___ once I approve the cost.
  3. 句式改写Combine using Provided that: The total stays within the agreed limit. You can start the repairs.
  4. 实际表达Write a 40–60-word message to a repair service. Describe a fault, ask what the callout fee includes, request a written estimate, and require approval before extra work. Use provided that to set a spending condition.
05

Answers

答案

展开参考答案
  1. 理解：1. The initial fee covered the visit and diagnosis. Parts and repair work would cost extra. 2. The technician should contact Maya before doing additional work. 3. Saving the estimate, invoice and receipt would make future follow-up easier.
  2. 词语：(a) under warranty; (b) written estimate; (c) scope; (d) go ahead.
  3. 句式改写：Provided that the total stays within the agreed limit, you can start the repairs.
  4. 实际表达参考：My washing machine stops before draining. Could you confirm what the callout fee includes and send me a written estimate? Provided that the total stays below the limit we agree on, you can go ahead with the repair. Please ask for my approval before carrying out any extra work.
