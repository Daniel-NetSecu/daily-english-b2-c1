Sorting Things Out When Plans Change

01

Vocabulary

生词和重点词语

跟进

回复某人

进度落后

We’re running slightly behind schedule.

及时告知最新进展

Please keep me posted on any changes.

配合某项限制安排；设法绕开困难

02

Reading

英语短文

Sorting Things Out When Plans Change

On Tuesday morning, Daniel received a message from the repair company saying that the engineer would arrive two hours later than expected. He had arranged to work from home, but the new time slot clashed with a meeting he was due to lead. Instead of cancelling everything, he called the company to explain the situation. “Could we move the appointment to Thursday morning?” he asked. “If that isn’t possible, I can make myself available after three today.” The receptionist checked the schedule and promised to get back to him within an hour. Daniel asked her to confirm the revised arrangement by email.

While waiting for a reply, Daniel followed up on a quotation from a supplier. His team was running behind schedule because they still needed confirmation of the delivery date. Rather than write “Any update?”, he explained what information was missing and why it mattered. “Could you confirm whether the equipment will arrive by Friday? We need to arrange access to the building and make sure someone is available to receive it.” The supplier replied that one item was out of stock but offered a suitable alternative. Daniel asked for the specifications and price before agreeing, then kept his colleagues posted on the change.

After work, Daniel stopped at a shop to return a faulty charger. He had brought the charger and the email receipt as proof of purchase. The assistant offered a replacement, but Daniel preferred a refund because he had already bought another one. Before leaving, he checked how long the refund would take to reach his account. He still had a few errands to run, but he decided to leave one for the following day. Not everything needed to be sorted out immediately. By asking clear questions and leaving some room for delays, he had managed a difficult day without making unrealistic promises.

03

Grammar

语法与沟通技巧

Could you confirm whether…?

礼貌确认信息；whether从句使用陈述语序，不写whether will the equipment arrive。

Example: Could you confirm whether the equipment will arrive by Friday?

be due to do

表示按计划将要做某事。The delivery is due to arrive tomorrow 表示预计到达；The delay was due to bad weather 中due to＋名词表示原因。

Example: He was due to lead a meeting.

before + 动词-ing

动作执行者与主句主语相同。执行者不同时用完整从句：Please check the details before the supplier ships the equipment.

Example: He asked for the specifications and price before agreeing.
