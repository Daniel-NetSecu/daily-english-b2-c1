Reading

英语短文 · 两天背诵版

When a Delivery Arrives at the Wrong Time

Home delivery is convenient until a parcel arrives when nobody is there to receive it. A message saying that a driver is nearby may seem helpful, but it leaves little time to change plans. Problems often arise because the sender, the driver, and the customer each assume that someone else has confirmed the arrangements. Clear communication can prevent a missed delivery from becoming a lost parcel. Instead of simply asking a driver to come later, it is better to explain the difficulty, check which alternatives are available, and agree on a specific next step. A convenient solution is only useful when everyone understands exactly what has been agreed.

Last week, Anna received a call while she was shopping across town. Her parcel required a signature, but the driver was already outside her building. “I am afraid I will not be home for another hour,” she explained. “Would it be possible to reschedule the delivery for tomorrow morning?” The driver could not promise a particular time and suggested leaving it with a neighbour. Anna declined because she had not asked anyone to accept it. “Could you take it to a collection point instead?” she asked. The driver told her to contact customer service, since he could not change the destination without authorisation. She thanked him and called the number shown in her order.

The customer service agent offered collection from a nearby shop the following afternoon. Before agreeing, Anna checked the opening hours, the collection deadline, and what identification she would need. “Please send me the address and collection instructions so that I can check them before I leave,” she said. The agent explained that she should wait for a notification confirming that the parcel was ready. Anna saved the reference number and resisted the temptation to visit the shop immediately. When the notification arrived the next day, she collected the parcel without difficulty. The original delivery had failed, but a few precise questions had prevented a second unnecessary journey.

03

Grammar & Patterns

语法与句型

主句 + so that + 主语 + can/could + 动词原形

so that 引导目的状语从句，表示“以便……”。说明提出请求的目的，让对方更容易理解。

Example: Please send me the address so that I can check it before I leave.
