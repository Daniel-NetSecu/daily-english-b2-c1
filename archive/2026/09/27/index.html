When Silence Speaks Across Cultures

沉默并非没有意义：理解不同文化中的停顿、尊重与沟通信号。

B2–C1约 10 分钟2026-09-27
01

Vocabulary

生词和重点词语

conversational norm n. phr.

交谈规范；某个群体中被普遍接受的交流方式

Conversational norms vary widely between cultures.

interpret v.

理解；诠释某种行为或信息的含义

People may interpret a long pause in very different ways.

convey v.

传达；表达思想、感受或态度

Silence can convey respect as clearly as words do.

rapport n.

融洽关系；相互理解与信任

Informal conversation can help colleagues build rapport.

implicit adj.

含蓄的；未直接说明但可以理解的

The manager’s disagreement was implicit rather than direct.

discomfort n.

不自在；不安或尴尬的感觉

She filled every pause because silence caused her discomfort.

withhold judgment v. phr.

暂不下判断；在了解更多情况前不作评价

It is wise to withhold judgment until you understand the local norms.

02

Reading

英语短文

When Silence Speaks Across Cultures

Imagine a meeting in which a manager asks for opinions and nobody answers immediately. One person may interpret the silence as a lack of preparation. Another may see it as a respectful pause that gives everyone time to think. Neither reaction is universal; each is shaped by conversational norms learned over many years.

In some cultures, quick responses convey confidence and help people build rapport. A pause that lasts several seconds may create discomfort, so speakers naturally fill it. In other cultures, interrupting a moment of silence can seem impatient. Careful listeners may wait before replying because they want their words to be accurate and considerate.

Silence can also communicate an implicit message. It may signal disagreement, uncertainty, respect for authority, or simply concentration. The difficulty is that the same behavior can carry opposite meanings in different settings. When people assume that their own interpretation is obvious, a quiet moment can easily become a source of mistrust.

Cross-cultural competence therefore requires curiosity rather than certainty. If a pause feels unusual, withhold judgment and look for additional clues: facial expressions, the relationship between speakers, and what happens next. You can also ask a neutral question such as, “Would you like a moment to think?” By making room for different communication styles, groups can turn silence from a barrier into a useful part of conversation.

03

Grammar & Patterns

语法与句型

The same behavior can be interpreted as A or B, depending on...

同一种行为可能被理解为 A 或 B，取决于……。被动语态 be interpreted as 强调行为所获得的不同解读；depending on 补充决定因素。

Example: The same silence can be interpreted as respect or uncertainty, depending on the context.

Rather than + doing..., + verb...

与其做……，不如……。rather than 后接动名词时，用于对比两种做法，并推荐后者。

Example: Rather than filling every pause, observe how others respond.
