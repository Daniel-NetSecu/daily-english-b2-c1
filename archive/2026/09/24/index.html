02

Reading

英语短文

More Than Saying Sorry

A good apology is not a performance designed to end an uncomfortable conversation. It is a way of showing that you understand another person’s experience. Saying “I’m sorry you feel that way” often fails because it focuses on the listener’s reaction rather than the speaker’s behavior. A sincere apology names the action and acknowledges its impact: “I’m sorry I interrupted you repeatedly. That was disrespectful.”

The next step is accountability. Explanations may provide context, but they should not become excuses. Compare “I was stressed, so I snapped at you” with “I was stressed, but that did not justify snapping at you.” The second sentence accepts responsibility while still explaining what happened. It also avoids becoming defensive or dismissive.

A thoughtful apology may express regret and propose a repair. You might say, “I wish I had listened more carefully. What can I do to make amends?” However, the person who was hurt is not required to forgive immediately. They may need time, and pressuring them can weaken the apology.

Finally, behavior matters more than perfect wording. If you promise to change but repeat the same action, trust will continue to decline. Rebuilding trust requires consistent evidence: listening without interrupting, keeping agreements, and correcting mistakes early. An apology opens the door to repair, but changed behavior is what allows the relationship to move through it.

03

Grammar & Patterns

语法与句型

I wish + subject + had + past participle

用于表达对过去行为的遗憾。wish 后用过去完成时，表示希望过去发生过不同的情况。

Example: I wish I had listened before giving advice.

take responsibility for + noun / -ing

表示“为……承担责任”。for 后接名词或动名词，不接动词原形。

Example: I take responsibility for making a decision without consulting you.
